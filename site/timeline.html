Key events

Milestones marked AUS come from Australian research or from sites in Australia and our region.

  • ~7 MaSahelanthropus in Chad: possibly the earliest hominin. Module 1
  • 4.4 Ma"Ardi" (Ardipithecus ramidus) lives in woodland in Ethiopia. Module 1
  • 3.66 MaLaetoli footprints record upright walking. Module 1
  • 3.3 MaOldest known stone tools, at Lomekwi 3, Kenya. Module 1
  • 3.2 Ma"Lucy" (Au. afarensis), Ethiopia. Module 1
  • 2.8 MaEarliest Homo jaw, Ledi-Geraru, Ethiopia. Module 2
  • ~2.6 MaAustralian-born Raymond Dart's Taung Child (Au. africanus), named 1925. AUS Module 1
  • ~2.1 MaStone tools at Shangchen, China: the earliest sign of hominins outside Africa. Module 2
  • ~2.0 MaThree hominin genera coexist at Drimolen and Malapa, South Africa (La Trobe-led research). AUS Module 1
  • 1.8 MaSmall-brained Homo at Dmanisi, Georgia. Module 2
  • 1.76 MaFirst Acheulean handaxes. Module 2
  • ≥1.04 MaHominins on Sulawesi (Calio) and Flores (Wolo Sege): the first crossings of the Wallace Line. AUS Module 2
  • ~1 MaEvidence of fire at Wonderwerk Cave, South Africa. Module 2
  • ~700 kaSmall-bodied hominins at Mata Menge, Flores. AUS Module 2
  • ~430 kaEarly Neanderthals at Sima de los Huesos, Spain. Module 3
  • ~315 kaEarliest Homo sapiens fossils, Jebel Irhoud, Morocco. Module 3
  • ~160 kaDenisovan jaw on the Tibetan Plateau (Xiahe). Module 3
  • 117–108 kaLast Homo erectus at Ngandong, Java (dating with Australian researchers). AUS Module 2
  • 100–60 kaHomo floresiensis remains at Liang Bua, Flores (Indonesian–Australian team). AUS Module 2
  • ~65 kaProposed earliest occupation of Sahul at Madjedbebe, Mirarr Country (debated). AUS Module 4
  • 60–50 kaMain dispersal of Homo sapiens out of Africa. Module 3
  • ≥51.2 kaSulawesi cave painting at Leang Karampuang (Griffith–Indonesian team). AUS Module 3
  • 50–45 kaMain period of interbreeding with Neanderthals. Module 3
  • ~50 kaPeople widely established across Sahul. AUS Module 4
  • ~42 kaMungo Lady and Mungo Man buried at Lake Mungo. AUS Module 4
  • ~40 kaNeanderthals disappear; most Sahul megafauna extinct. Module 4
  • 23–20 kaWillandra Lakes footprints. AUS Module 4
  • ~8 kaRising seas separate New Guinea from Australia. AUS Module 4
  • ≥6.6 kaGunditjmara eel aquaculture at Budj Bim. AUS Module 4
  • 2017Mungo Man returned to Country. AUS Module 4

Dates are approximate and rounded from published studies. See Sources for references. Content current to November 2026.
