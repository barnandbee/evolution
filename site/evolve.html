Ages of Evolution

Collect the Ages. Evolve. Survive.

You begin about 8 million years ago as the last common ancestor of chimpanzees and humans. Travel through six eras of the Module 1 story. Each era has a glowing Age Stone: answer its question to evolve into the next species and open the way forward.

MoveArrow keys or WASD, or the on-screen arrows.
CollectStep on an Age Stone (◆) to answer its question. Find the stone tools to break boulders.
SurviveSnakes, an eagle and big cats drain your energy. Eat fruit, tubers and marrow to recover.
ChooseAt about 2.4 million years ago the path forks. Will you specialise, or stay a generalist?

The game is a simplified, single path through a branching family tree; real relationships between these species are debated. It is optional and does not affect module completion. A wrong answer costs one energy, and you can always try again.
